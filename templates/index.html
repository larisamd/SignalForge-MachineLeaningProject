PHISHING RESILIENCE

Before you trust a message

01
Verify the sender independently

Inspect the full address. Contact the person or organization using a known channel.

02
Preview the real link destination

Check the hostname carefully. HTTPS encrypts a connection; it does not prove a site is trustworthy.

03
Resist urgency and unexpected rewards

Pause on threats, payment demands, password requests and too-good-to-be-true prizes.

04
Use MFA and a password manager

Unique passwords limit reuse risk; phishing-resistant passkeys or security keys are stronger where supported.

05
Report suspicious messages

Use your organization's reporting process. Do not forward a suspicious link by clicking it.
